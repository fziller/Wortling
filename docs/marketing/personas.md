# Wortkniff Marketing Personas

These personas keep app-store copy, screenshots, and feature decisions focused. They are not product requirements.

## Primary Personas

### 1. Daily Puzzle Player

**Motivation:** Wants one small, satisfying puzzle every day.

**Context:** Plays during breakfast, commute, lunch break, or on the sofa. Knows games like Wordle, Connections, Mini Crossword, or Sudoku apps.

**What sells Wortkniff:**

- A fresh German daily puzzle routine.
- Several short word games in one app.
- No account, no setup, no endless grind.
- Progress, streaks, and shareable results.

**Likely search terms:** Wortspiel, Worträtsel, tägliches Rätsel, Daily Puzzle, Wordle Deutsch, Wörterspiel.

**Tone:** Clear, quick, rewarding.

**Avoid:** Long explanations, competitive pressure, heavy learning language.

### 2. German Word Nerd

**Motivation:** Enjoys clever language, word relations, compound words, and small aha moments.

**Context:** Likes German-specific mechanics instead of translated English word games.

**What sells Wortkniff:**

- Built around German words, compounds, and meanings.
- More varied than one Wordle clone.
- Curated puzzles instead of random dictionary noise.
- Feels smart without feeling academic.

**Likely search terms:** deutsche Wörter, Komposita, Wortschatz, Wortquiz, Sprachspiel, Buchstabenrätsel.

**Tone:** Clever, precise, slightly playful.

**Avoid:** Generic brain-training claims, school/test framing, dry linguistics.

### 3. Casual Break Player

**Motivation:** Wants a quick mental snack with almost no friction.

**Context:** Does not want tutorials, accounts, timers, or complex rules.

**What sells Wortkniff:**

- Open the app, tap today's puzzle, play.
- Each mode is explainable in one sentence.
- Rounds take about 2 to 5 minutes.
- Works offline.

**Likely search terms:** kostenloses Wortspiel, kurzes Spiel, Rätsel App, Buchstaben Spiel, Offline Spiel.

**Tone:** Simple, friendly, low-pressure.

**Avoid:** Too much strategy language, too many mode names upfront, niche references.

### 4. Streak And Share Player

**Motivation:** Likes the daily ritual and the small social proof of finishing.

**Context:** Compares results casually with friends or family, but does not need a social network.

**What sells Wortkniff:**

- Same daily puzzles for everyone.
- Personal stats and streaks.
- Results are easy to share.
- Multiple daily wins instead of one-and-done.

**Likely search terms:** tägliches Wortspiel, tägliche Challenge, Streak, Rätsel teilen, Wordle Alternative.

**Tone:** Motivating, daily, satisfying.

**Avoid:** Leaderboards, FOMO, pushy retention wording.

## Positioning Summary

Wortkniff is for people who want short, polished, German-first word puzzles as a daily habit. It should feel clever and modern, not like a generic brain-training app.

## Messaging Rules

- Lead with daily German word puzzles.
- Mention variety early: multiple modes, one app.
- Keep rules approachable: short rounds, simple start, quick payoff.
- Use German-specific value: words, compounds, alphabet, meaning.
- Say offline/no account only as trust points, not as the main headline.
- Avoid overclaiming cognitive benefits.

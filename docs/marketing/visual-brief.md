# Wortkniff Visual Brief

Use this before creating app icons, splash screens, screenshots, or Stitch explorations.

## Desired Feel

- Clever, German-first, polished.
- Friendly and colorful, but not childish.
- Modern daily puzzle app, not generic brain training.
- Calm enough for a daily routine, distinct enough to remember.

## Visual Keywords

- Word tiles
- Letter logic
- Daily ritual
- German compounds
- Search space narrowing
- Clear typography
- Soft motion
- Warm contrast

## Icon Direction Candidates

### 1. Wordmark Tile

A bold `W` inside a rounded tile. Simple, legible, scalable.

### 2. Between Motif

A `W` or letter tile framed by two subtle arrows/brackets, hinting at narrowing a word range.

### 3. Compound Link

Two letter blocks connected by a small bridge shape, hinting at Komposita and word relationships.

### 4. Daily Card

A bright rounded square with one strong letter and a tiny calendar/dot motif. More daily-routine oriented.

## Splash Screen Direction

Keep it boring in the good way:

- Solid brand background.
- Centered icon or simple Wortkniff wordmark.
- No illustration.
- No loading text unless needed.

## Avoid

- Dictionary/book icons.
- Brain icons.
- Generic crossword grids.
- Too many tiny letters.
- German flag colors as the main identity.
- Overly close Wordle styling.
